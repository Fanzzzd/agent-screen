While an agent uses your mouse or keyboard, the edges of your screens now glow red: a solid rim with a wide soft glow that tints the screen's edge, strongest while input goes out and steady while the agent may still use them. It replaces the thin red line, which was easy to miss. The glow also shows when an agent types through your input method, with the bar at the bottom of the screen naming the agent and offering Stop, and when "Let the agent continue" briefly takes the front. It lets every click through, never appears in an agent's screenshots, and goes away the moment control is back with you.
