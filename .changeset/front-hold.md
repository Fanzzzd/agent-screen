An action that waits for you to leave your browser no longer needs you to switch apps. Beside "Waits for you to leave Dia", on the display's tile in Peek, in the Viewer header, and in the Input Queue card under the menu bar icon when it is open, "Let the agent continue" runs the waiting actions now. Agent Screen stands in front of your browser for the moment they take, then puts your browser back in front with your window focused. Keys you type meanwhile go to your window after; typing or clicking stops what is left, which waits again.
