`place_window` now brings a window you minimized to the Dock, or a window of an app you hid, onto the agent's display, once you have told the agent to take it or answered Hand Over. Before, it answered that no window was on screen, and you had to restore the window yourself first. When the window is closed or on another macOS Space, the agent is now told which, so it knows to ask you to bring it up.
