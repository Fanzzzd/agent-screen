You can now choose how much agents may do without asking, in Settings and from the menu bar. Ask is what Agent Screen did before: agents ask for a window, your mouse or your keyboard. In Auto, agents take windows you haven't used for two minutes and use your mouse and keyboard once you've left them alone for five seconds, and they stop the moment you touch either; what you typed in that moment still reaches your window. In YOLO they take them at once, even while you work. Press Control-Option-Command-Period, or Stop, to end it and go back to Ask. YOLO turns itself off when your Mac locks or sleeps, and the menu bar icon stays red while it is on. In every mode, password fields, system prompts, password managers and System Settings stay out, a window you took back stays yours, and the red glow shows whenever an agent has your mouse or keyboard.
