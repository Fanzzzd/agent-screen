A window that left its Agent Display during a Takeover for a spot where almost none of it shows is put back when you hand the display back. A report showed the agents' Chrome window jumping above the main display while the user held the display, with only 30 points of it showing, out of sight for both the user and the agent. Only a window with almost nothing on any display goes back: less than 100 points wide, 40 points high, or a tenth of the window. One you dragged onto your own screen or another display, or parked at a screen's edge with a good part showing, stays where you put it.
