When you bring up an app whose windows are all on Agent Displays, Peek now drops down from the menu bar icon and shows where they are: that display's tile glows orange with the app's windows outlined. Click the tile to open it in the Viewer, or Take Back to bring the windows to your screen. Peek goes after 5 seconds unless your pointer is on it. Clicking the app's Dock icon does this too, also when the app is already the front app; before, that click did nothing and the window looked lost.
