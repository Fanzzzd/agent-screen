When macOS mirrors your screen onto a new display, which makes your screen take that display's resolution, Agent Screen now turns the mirror off, parks the display beside your screen, and has macOS remember that arrangement. Your screen flashes once, for about a second, and the agent keeps its display. The next display of that size, with the same displays connected, lands beside your screen with no flash. Agent Screen releases the display and pauses new displays for up to 30 minutes, as in 0.1.39, only when the mirror can't be turned off or comes back after macOS stored the arrangement.
